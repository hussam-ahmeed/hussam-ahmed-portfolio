A quick introduction

I'm a senior cybersecurity student at Sana'a University, graduating in 2025. My focus is on practical, hands-on learning — I build isolated home labs to practice offensive security techniques, then document every finding clearly and responsibly.

I started with networking (CCNA) and Python scripting, which gave me a solid foundation before diving into penetration testing. I believe good security work starts with understanding how systems communicate and where they can break.
